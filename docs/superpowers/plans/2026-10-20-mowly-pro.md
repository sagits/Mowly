# Mowly Pro (Provider Track) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build every Mowly Pro screen on top of the foundation:
- the dashboard checklist with all 8 steps (ticket 2)
- Available Jobs with claim, Calendar and Route map (ticket 3)
- Settings, sign out, welcome and sign in (ticket 4)
- then sign up, Recommend a service, Support, the invented drawer screens and the post-checklist dashboard

**Architecture:** Screens live in `apps/pro/app` and app-only pieces in `apps/pro/src`. Signed-in screens sit under `app/(app)/` inside the foundation's drawer shell, with a native `Stack` inside it. Checklist progress is **derived from the `Pro` record** in the mock database (`checklistDone(pro)`), not kept in a separate store. That way a step finished from Settings and the same step finished from the dashboard are one fact. Record this in `DECISIONS.md` as a deviation from the PRD's "onboarding progress in Zustand". Every step screen saves through `useUpdatePro` and then goes back, so it works the same from the dashboard and from Settings.

**Tech Stack:** As the foundation plan. `react-native-svg` for the city map and route line.

**Spec:** `PRD.md` ("Features: pro", "Design", "Navigation"), the screenshots `screenshots/providers-app/NN-*` (P01 to P25), and `prototype/index.html` for invented screens.

**Prerequisite:** The foundation plan is complete on `master`. This plan runs in its own worktree on branch `track/pro` (`superpowers:using-git-worktrees`), at the same time as the customer track. Run `pnpm install` once in the worktree. Create this track's simulator with `xcrun simctl create "iPhone 16 Mowly Pro" "iPhone 16"`, then build in the background with `E2E_DEVICE="iPhone 16 Mowly Pro" pnpm --filter @mowly/pro e2e:build`.

## Review Focus

- **Double-tapping CLAIM JOB** must claim once. The button is disabled while pending, and a second claim of a claimed job shows a toast, not a crash. Tested in Task 6
- **Tapping a locked checklist row** must do nothing. Tested in Task 1
- **After signing out, the back gesture or the browser Back button** must not re-enter the app. `(app)/_layout` redirects whenever `signedIn` is false. Tested in Task 11
- **The calendar across a month boundary:** today's outline sits in the right month, the next month's grid starts on the right weekday, and counts land on the right days. Tested in Task 7
- **A job claimed for today** appears last on today's route with the "from previous job" label, while the first stop keeps "from starting point". Tested in Task 8
